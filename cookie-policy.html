What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device — whether a computer, tablet, or smartphone — when you visit a website. They are widely used across the internet to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide website owners with valuable insights into how visitors interact with their content.

A cookie typically contains the name of the website from which it originated, a unique identifier, and a period of time that the cookie remains active on your device. Cookies cannot retrieve any other data from your device or deliver viruses to your computer. They are simply small, safe data files designed to enhance your online experience.

At Divine Master, we use cookies to remember your theme preference, understand which pages attract the most engagement, identify any technical issues, and ensure that your visit feels smooth, elegant, and personalised — consistent with the premium experience we aim to deliver in every interaction.

Types of Cookies

Cookies can be classified in several ways — by their lifespan, by the party that sets them, and by their purpose. Understanding these categories helps you make informed decisions about the cookies you allow when visiting our website.

Session Cookies are temporary cookies that remain on your device only while your browser is open. They are deleted automatically when you close your browser. They help the website remember your actions on a single visit, such as items displayed or texts entered, so that you do not have to re-enter them.

Persistent Cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them manually. They remember your preferences across multiple visits, such as your chosen light or dark theme, and allow us to recognise you as a returning visitor.

First-Party Cookies are set directly by Divine Master and are used only by us. Third-Party Cookies are placed by our trusted partners or service providers, such as analytics platforms, and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the basic operation of the Divine Master website. Without these cookies, certain core functions — such as secure navigation, session management, and the ability to move through different parts of the site — would not function correctly.

These cookies do not collect information that identifies you personally. They are technical in nature and are generally stored in one of two categories: strictly necessary cookies, which are required for the website to function, and preference cookies, which remember choices you make about how the website behaves.

Because essential cookies are required for the website to work, they do not require your prior consent. However, we are transparent about their use and provide clear information about them so that you may make an informed decision about your browsing experience.

  • Maintain secure and authenticated sessions across the website.
  • Remember navigation preferences and section selections.
  • Preserve your chosen theme (light or dark) between visits.
  • Ensure consistent functionality across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the Divine Master website by collecting anonymous, aggregated information such as the number of visitors, the pages most frequently viewed, the average time spent on each page, and the general geographic regions from which visitors access our site.

These insights are invaluable. They allow us to identify which content resonates most deeply with our community, where visitors may experience confusion or encounter issues, and how we can improve the structure, clarity, and elegance of the website.

The data collected by analytics cookies is aggregated and anonymised. It does not identify you personally, and we do not use this information to build profiles of individual visitors. We may use trusted analytics providers, such as Google Analytics, who process this data in accordance with their own privacy policies and our strict data processing agreements.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the Divine Master website to remember choices you have made and to provide enhanced, more personal features. By retaining simple preferences, these cookies ensure that your visits feel familiar, seamless, and tailored to the way you prefer to engage with our content.

For example, if you switch the theme to dark mode, a functional cookie remembers that preference so that the next time you visit, you are welcomed with the theme you have chosen. Similarly, if you have viewed certain sections or engaged with particular resources, functional cookies can help us present related content in a helpful way.

Functional cookies do not track your activity on other websites. Their scope is limited to enhancing your experience on the Divine Master platform. Disabling functional cookies may result in a less personalised experience, but the core functionality of the website will continue to operate.

Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are used to deliver content that is relevant and useful to you. Where we use these cookies — always with your consent — they may help us understand which of our services or journal articles may be of interest based on the pages you visit within our website.

We do not engage in aggressive or intrusive advertising practices. Any marketing-related cookies are used with restraint and respect, and we are transparent about the partners with whom such data may be shared. You may withdraw your consent for marketing cookies at any time through your browser settings.

We believe in quality over quantity in all aspects of our communications. If you choose to allow marketing cookies, we will ensure that any advertising or informational content you encounter remains aligned with the refined, premium nature of the Divine Master brand.

Managing Cookies

You have the right and the ability to control which cookies are placed on your device. The Divine Master website is designed to function effectively even when non-essential cookies are disabled, and we encourage you to make the choices that feel right for your personal level of comfort.

When you first visit our website, where required by law, we present you with a cookie consent notice that explains the types of cookies we use. You may accept all cookies, accept only essential cookies, or customise your preferences. Your choices are respected, and you may change them at any time.

If you wish to withdraw consent after granting it, you can delete cookies from your browser or adjust your browser settings to block new cookies from being placed. Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact aspects of the website's functionality and your overall experience.

Browser Settings

Most modern web browsers give you control over cookies through their settings menus. You can typically choose to block all cookies, accept all cookies, receive a notification before a cookie is placed, or delete individual cookies that have already been stored on your device.

Because each browser presents these settings differently, we encourage you to consult the help or support section of your specific browser for detailed guidance. The steps for managing cookies in popular browsers are described broadly below, though the exact wording and location of settings may vary.

  • Google Chrome: Navigate to Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Navigate to Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Navigate to Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Navigate to Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Site data.

If you choose to disable cookies entirely, certain features of the Divine Master website may not function as intended. In particular, your theme preference may not be remembered between visits, and some interactive elements may require you to re-submit information more frequently.
